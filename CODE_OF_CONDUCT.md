# Code of conduct

## The standard

Everyone taking part in this project — issues, pull requests, reviews, email —
is expected to behave professionally and in good faith. Harassment, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, and sustained disruption are not acceptable
and will be dealt with.

This project takes the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1 as its basis. It is referenced rather than reproduced here so that the
canonical text, and any correction to it, stays the authority.

## What this means for a research artefact

Most of what happens in this repository is disagreement about measurements, and
that is the point of it. The project exists because a widely used benchmark
turned out to measure something other than what it claimed, and several of the
defects recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) are ones found in this corpus
by outside review or by the authors auditing their own work.

So, to be explicit:

- **Saying a result is wrong is not an attack.** Showing that a number does not
  reproduce, that a split leaks, or that the gate misses a shortcut is the most
  useful thing anyone can do here.
- **Say it about the work, not the person.** "This measurement does not hold,
  here is the evidence" is welcome in any tone. "You are incompetent" is not,
  however well-founded the underlying point.
- **Bring the measurement.** A claim that something is broken, with the seeds,
  the versions and the numbers, is a contribution. The same claim without them
  is an assertion, and will be asked for evidence before anything else.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to anishc2301@gmail.com. Reports will be read by the
maintainers, kept confidential, and acted on — which may mean a warning, or
blocking an account from the repository.

Do not use this address for scientific disputes about the corpus; those belong
in a public issue, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) sets out. Security
problems in the repository itself go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in the repository and in any project space, and to conduct
elsewhere that is directed at a participant because of their involvement here.
